About BloodLink

BloodLink Rwanda is a platform designed to make it easier for people searching for blood donors to connect with willing donors based on blood type and district.

How BloodLink Works

1. Search

Choose the blood type and district you need.

2. Connect

Find available donor contact information.

3. Help

Contact the donor to discuss the next steps.
